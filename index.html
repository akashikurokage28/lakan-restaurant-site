Our story

Where smoke meets heritage.

Lakan began with one idea: that the dishes of our grandmothers deserve a white tablecloth. Every plate starts over open charcoal and ends with something familiar, made precise.

  • Open charcoalEvery grill dish is cooked over coconut-shell charcoal.
  • Island produceFish, rice and vegetables from growers across Panay.
  • Made by handStocks, sauces and vinegars are prepared in our kitchen daily.
